## 🧭 What Inbound Students Must Do

Inbound students must complete the following steps:

1. Submit required visa documents  
2. Purchase approved travel & health insurance  
3. Confirm housing arrangements  
4. Submit arrival information (flight details)  
5. Attend orientation sessions  
6. Register emergency contacts  
7. Review Japanese laws, customs, and safety guidelines  

These steps ensure compliance with university policies and smooth arrival procedures.

## 🏠 Housing & Arrival Support

Inbound students can choose from:

- University dormitories  
- Private apartments  
- Homestay options  

Arrival support includes:

- Airport pickup (if available)  
- Move‑in guidance  
- Local city registration support  
- Opening a bank account  
- SIM card / mobile phone setup assistance  

---

## 🛡 Safety & Local Regulations

Inbound students must:

- Follow Japanese laws and university rules  
- Carry residence card at all times  
- Register at the city hall within 14 days of arrival  
- Maintain valid insurance coverage  
- Report incidents promptly to the International Office  

Safety is our top priority.
