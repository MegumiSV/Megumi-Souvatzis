
# 📄 Required Documents for Inbound Students

Inbound students must submit the following documents before arriving in Japan.  
These documents ensure smooth visa processing, arrival coordination, and compliance with university and government requirements.

Please review each item carefully and submit them by the deadlines provided by the International Student Office.

---

## 📝 Document Checklist

### 1. **Passport Copy**
A clear scan of your passport photo page.  
Must be valid for the entire duration of your stay in Japan.

### 2. **Certificate of Eligibility (COE)**
Issued by the university and required for visa application.  
Submit a digital copy once received.

### 3. **Visa Application Documents**
Submit copies of:
- Visa application form  
- Visa issuance confirmation (after approval)  
- Any additional documents required by the Japanese Embassy/Consulate  

### 4. **Acceptance Letter**
Official admission letter from the university.  
Required for immigration and arrival procedures.

### 5. **Insurance Certificate**
Proof of approved travel and health insurance.  
Must include:
- Medical coverage  
- Hospitalization  
- Emergency evacuation  
- Personal liability  

### 6. **Arrival Information Form**
Submit your confirmed flight itinerary, including:
- Departure and arrival dates  
- Airline and flight numbers  
- Transit locations  

### 7. **Emergency Contact Sheet**
Provide:
- Primary emergency contact  
- Secondary contact  
- Local contact (if applicable)  
→ `emergency-contacts.md`

---

## 📂 How to Submit Documents

Documents should be uploaded to the designated submission folder or sent via the university’s secure portal.

Accepted formats:
- PDF  
- JPEG/PNG  

Ensure all files are clearly readable.

---

## ⏰ Deadlines

All required documents must be submitted **at least 30 days before arrival** unless otherwise specified.  
Late submissions may delay visa processing or arrival support.

---

## 🛡 Why These Documents Matter

These documents help the university:
- Process visa and immigration requirements  
- Prepare arrival support  
- Coordinate housing and orientation  
- Ensure student safety and compliance  

Submitting complete and accurate documents is essential for a smooth arrival experience.
