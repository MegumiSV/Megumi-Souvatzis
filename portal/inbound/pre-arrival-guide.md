
# 🌏 Pre‑Arrival Guide for Inbound Students

This guide provides essential steps for international students preparing to arrive in Japan.  
Please follow each step carefully to ensure a smooth arrival and transition to campus life.

---

## 🧭 Step-by-Step Preparation

### 1. **Confirm Your Visa Documents**
Ensure you have:
- Certificate of Eligibility (COE)  
- Passport (valid for the entire study period)  
- Visa issued by the Japanese Embassy/Consulate  

Keep digital copies of all documents.

---

### 2. **Purchase Approved Insurance**
Inbound students must have valid travel and health insurance covering:
- Medical treatment  
- Hospitalization  
- Emergency evacuation  
- Personal liability  

Submit your insurance certificate before arrival.

---

### 3. **Arrange Housing**
Choose from:
- University dormitories  
- Private apartments  
- Homestay options  

You will receive move‑in instructions once housing is confirmed.

---

### 4. **Submit Arrival Information**
Provide:
- Flight itinerary  
- Arrival date and time  
- Airport (Narita, Haneda, Kansai, etc.)  
- Emergency contact information  

This helps the university coordinate arrival support.

---

### 5. **Prepare for Life in Japan**
Before arrival, review:
- Local customs and etiquette  
- Basic Japanese phrases  
- Transportation options  
- Weather and clothing recommendations  

---

### 6. **Bring Required Documents**
You must carry:
- Passport  
- Visa  
- COE  
- Acceptance letter  
- Insurance certificate  
- Emergency contact sheet  

Keep them in your carry‑on luggage.

---

## 🛬 Arrival Procedures in Japan

### 🏢 1. Immigration
Present:
- Passport  
- Visa  
- COE  
- Arrival card  

You will receive a **Residence Card** at major airports.

---

### 🏙 2. City Hall Registration
Within **14 days**, register your address at the local city hall.  
You will receive:
- My Number notification  
- National Health Insurance enrollment (if required)

---

### 📱 3. Mobile Phone & Banking
Support is available for:
- SIM card setup  
- Opening a bank account  
- Setting up online payment apps

---

## 🎒 What to Bring

- Comfortable clothing for all seasons  
- Medication (with prescriptions)  
- Laptop and chargers  
- International credit card  
- Emergency contact list  
- Important documents (passport, COE, visa)
