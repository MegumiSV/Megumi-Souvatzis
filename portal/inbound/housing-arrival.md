# 🏠 Housing & Arrival Support for Inbound Students

This page provides essential information about housing options, move‑in procedures, and arrival support for international students coming to Japan.

Our goal is to ensure that inbound students have a comfortable living environment and a smooth transition upon arrival.

---

## 🏡 Housing Options

Inbound students may choose from the following housing types:

### 1. **University Dormitories**
Affordable and convenient housing located near campus.  
Features:
- Furnished rooms  
- Shared kitchen and lounge  
- On‑site support staff  
- Wi‑Fi included  

### 2. **Private Apartments**
Suitable for students who prefer independent living.  
Features:
- Monthly rent varies by location  
- Utilities may be separate  
- Contracts typically require a guarantor  

### 3. **Homestay**
Live with a Japanese host family.  
Features:
- Cultural immersion  
- Daily language practice  
- Meals included (depending on host family)  

---

## 📦 Move‑In Procedures

### 🗓 1. Move‑In Date
You will receive your official move‑in date after housing is confirmed.  
Please do not arrive earlier than the designated date.

### 🧳 2. What to Bring
- Passport  
- Residence card (if already issued)  
- Housing contract  
- Bedding (or purchase locally)  
- Personal toiletries  
- SIM card or mobile phone  

### 🧼 3. Room Setup
Dormitory rooms typically include:
- Bed  
- Desk & chair  
- Storage space  
- Air conditioner  

Students may need to purchase:
- Bedding  
- Kitchen supplies  
- Cleaning items  

---

## 🛬 Arrival Support

### ✈️ 1. Airport Arrival
Support may include:
- Airport pickup (if available)  
- Guidance on transportation to campus  
- Emergency contact information  

### 🏙 2. City Hall Registration
Within **14 days** of arrival, students must:
- Register their address  
- Enroll in National Health Insurance (if required)  

University staff may assist with this process.

### 📱 3. Mobile Phone & SIM Card
Support is available for:
- SIM card setup  
- Mobile plan selection  
- Troubleshooting connectivity issues  

### 🏦 4. Bank Account Setup
Students may receive assistance with:
- Opening a bank account  
- Setting up online banking  
- Understanding ATM usage  

---

## 🧭 Local Living Tips

- Convenience stores are open 24/7  
- Cash is still widely used in Japan  
- Public transportation is reliable and punctual  
- Learn basic Japanese phrases for daily life  
- Keep your residence card with you at all times
