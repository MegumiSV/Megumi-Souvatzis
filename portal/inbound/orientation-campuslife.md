# 🎓 Orientation & Campus Life for Inbound Students

This page provides essential information about orientation, campus resources, and daily life for international students studying in Japan.

Our goal is to help inbound students feel welcomed, supported, and fully integrated into campus life.

---

## 🗓 Orientation Schedule

Orientation is held during the first week after arrival.  
It includes:

### 1. **Welcome Session**
- Introduction to university staff  
- Overview of academic programs  
- Campus tour  

### 2. **Administrative Procedures**
- City hall registration  
- National Health Insurance enrollment  
- Bank account setup  
- Student ID card issuance  

### 3. **Academic Guidance**
- Course registration  
- Syllabus explanation  
- Classroom etiquette  
- Japanese grading system overview  

### 4. **Safety & Emergency Training**
- Earthquake and disaster preparedness  
- Campus safety rules  
- Emergency contact procedures

## 🎉 Student Life & Activities

Inbound students are encouraged to join:

- Student clubs & circles  
- Cultural exchange events  
- Weekend trips  
- Volunteer activities  
- International student meetups  

These activities help students make friends and experience Japanese culture.

---

## 🍱 Daily Life Tips

- Convenience stores are open 24/7  
- Cash is still widely used  
- Public transportation is punctual  
- Learn basic Japanese phrases  
- Keep your residence card with you at all times  
- Download useful apps (maps, translation, transit)
