
# 🛫 Flight Ticket Submission

All outbound students must submit their confirmed flight itinerary before departure.  
This information is required for emergency coordination, travel monitoring, and communication with partner universities.

Please review the instructions below and submit your flight details by the specified deadline.

---

## 📄 What to Submit

Your flight ticket submission must include:

### 1. **Departure Information**
- Departure airport  
- Date and time  
- Airline  
- Flight number  

### 2. **Arrival Information**
- Arrival airport  
- Date and time  
- Airline  
- Flight number  

### 3. **Transit / Layover Details**
If your itinerary includes transit locations, provide:
- Transit airport  
- Duration  
- Connecting flight numbers  

### 4. **Full Itinerary Document**
Upload one of the following:
- PDF of your e-ticket  
- Screenshot of your booking confirmation  
- Photo of your itinerary (must be readable)

---

## 📤 How to Submit

Submit your flight ticket through the designated portal or upload it to the document submission folder.

Accepted formats:
- PDF  
- JPEG/PNG  

Ensure the file is clearly readable.

---

## ⏰ Deadline

Flight ticket information must be submitted **at least 14 days before departure**.

Late submissions may delay coordination with partner universities.

---

## 🛡 Why This Is Required

Flight information helps the university:
- Track student travel safely  
- Provide emergency support  
- Coordinate arrival information with partner institutions  
- Confirm insurance coverage periods  

Accurate and timely submission is essential for a smooth study abroad experience.
