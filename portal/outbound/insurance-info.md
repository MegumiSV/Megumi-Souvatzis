
# 🛡 Insurance Information for Outbound Students

All outbound students must purchase approved travel insurance before departure.  
This insurance ensures safety, emergency support, and compliance with university and partner institution requirements.

Please review the coverage details carefully and confirm that your insurance meets all mandatory criteria.

---

## 📘 Required Coverage

Your insurance plan **must include** the following:

### 1. **Medical Coverage**
Covers hospitalization, outpatient treatment, prescriptions, and emergency care.

### 2. **Emergency Evacuation**
Provides transportation to the nearest medical facility in case of severe illness or injury.

### 3. **Repatriation**
Covers transportation back to your home country if medically necessary.

### 4. **Personal Liability**
Protects you in case you accidentally cause injury or damage to property.

### 5. **Accident & Injury Coverage**
Includes compensation for accidental injuries during your stay abroad.

---

## 🧾 Recommended Additional Coverage

Although not mandatory, the following coverage is strongly recommended:

- Lost or stolen baggage  
- Travel delays  
- Passport replacement  
- Personal belongings protection  
- Mental health support services  

---

## 📝 Proof of Insurance

Students must submit:

- A digital copy of the insurance certificate (PDF or image)  
- A summary of coverage (if provided by the insurer)  
- Emergency hotline numbers  

→ Submit via `required-documents.md`

---

## 🚨 Emergency Procedures

Your insurance provider must offer:

- 24/7 emergency hotline  
- English-language support  
- Clear instructions for claims  
- Local assistance in your host country  

Keep these details accessible at all times during your stay abroad.

---

## 💡 How to Choose an Insurance Plan

When selecting a plan, ensure:

- Coverage meets university requirements  
- Claims can be filed online  
- Support is available internationally  
- Coverage is valid for the entire duration of your stay  

If unsure, consult the Global Mobility Office.

---

## ⏰ Deadline

Insurance must be purchased and submitted **at least 30 days before departure**.

Late submissions may result in delays or cancellation of participation.
