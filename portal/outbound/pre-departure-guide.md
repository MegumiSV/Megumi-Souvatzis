# Pre‑Departure Guide

### 🎯 Purpose  
This guide outlines all required actions students must complete before departing Japan for outbound programs. Follow each step carefully to ensure compliance, safety, and smooth preparation.

---

## 🧭 1. Confirm Program Details  
Before preparing documents, verify the following:

- Official program acceptance  
- Host institution information  
- Program duration and schedule  
- Contact details for coordinators  
- Required academic or training components  

---

## 🛫 2. Finalize Travel Arrangements  
Students must secure and confirm their travel plans:

- Book round‑trip flight tickets  
- Confirm arrival date and time  
- Review transit
