
# Outbound Students Overview

### 🎯 Overview  
This page provides a clear, structured summary of all required steps for outbound program participants. It outlines mandatory tasks, documents, and safety procedures to ensure compliance and smooth preparation before departure.

---

## 🧭 1. Pre‑Departure Essentials  
Key actions students must complete before leaving Japan:

- Review program requirements  
- Confirm travel dates and destination  
- Attend mandatory orientation  
- Prepare required documents  
- Arrange approved insurance coverage  

Next step → `/portal/outbound/pre-departure-guide.md`

---

## 📄 2. Required Documentation  
All outbound students must submit the following items:

- Pledge form  
- Insurance certificate  
- Flight itinerary  
- Emergency contact information  
- Program acceptance documents  

Next step → `/portal/outbound/required-documents.md`

---

## 🛡 3. Insurance Compliance  
Students must enroll in insurance plans that meet institutional and host‑country requirements:

- Medical emergency coverage  
- Liability insurance  
- Travel accident insurance  
- Submission of insurance certificate  

Next step → `/portal/outbound/insurance-info.md`

---

## 🛫 4. Flight Itinerary Submission  
Students must submit their confirmed flight details:

- Upload flight ticket PDF  
- Provide arrival date and time  
- Confirm transit details  
- Update itinerary if changes occur  

Next step → `/portal/outbound/flight-ticket-submission.md`

---

## 🆘 5. Emergency Contact Registration  
Students must register complete emergency contact information:

- Primary contact (family)  
- Secondary contact  
- Local contact (host institution)  
- Embassy/consulate details  

Next step → `/portal/outbound/emergency-contacts.md`

---

## 📌 6. Program Structure Summary  
Outbound programs typically include:

- Pre‑departure orientation  
- Host institution onboarding  
- Academic or training activities  
- Regular check‑ins  
- Final reporting upon return
