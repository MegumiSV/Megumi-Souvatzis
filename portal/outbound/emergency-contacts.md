# 🆘 Emergency Contacts for Outbound Students

All outbound students must provide emergency contact information before departure.  
This information is essential for safety, crisis response, and coordination with partner universities.

Please complete the required fields and submit your emergency contact details by the specified deadline.

---

## 📄 Required Emergency Contacts

### 1. **Primary Emergency Contact**
Your main point of contact in case of emergencies.
Include:
- Full name  
- Relationship  
- Phone number  
- Email address  
- Home address  

### 2. **Secondary Emergency Contact**
A backup contact if the primary contact cannot be reached.
Include:
- Full name  
- Relationship  
- Phone number  
- Email address  

### 3. **Local Contact (If Applicable)**
If you already know someone in your host country (family, friend, host family), include:
- Full name  
- Relationship  
- Local phone number  
- Local address  

---

## 🛡 Why Emergency Contacts Are Required

Emergency contacts allow the university to:
- Notify family in case of urgent situations  
- Coordinate support during medical emergencies  
- Provide accurate information to partner institutions  
- Ensure student safety throughout the program  

Accurate and up‑to‑date information is essential.

---

## 📤 How to Submit

Submit your emergency contact sheet through the designated portal or upload it to the document submission folder.

Accepted formats:
- PDF  
- JPEG/PNG  

Ensure all information is clearly readable.

---

## ⏰ Deadline

Emergency contact information must be submitted **at least 30 days before departure**.

Late submissions may delay your program approval.
