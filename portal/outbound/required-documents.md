
# Required Documents

### 🎯 Purpose  
This page outlines all mandatory documents students must submit before departing Japan for outbound programs. Ensure all items are completed and submitted by the designated deadlines.

---

## 📘 1. Core Submission Items  
Students must prepare and submit the following documents:

- **Pledge Form**  
  Agreement to comply with program rules, safety guidelines, and institutional policies.

- **Insurance Certificate**  
  Proof of enrollment in approved medical, liability, and travel insurance plans.

- **Flight Itinerary**  
  Confirmed round‑trip flight details including dates, times, and transit information.

- **Emergency Contact Information**  
  Primary, secondary, and local contacts for use during emergencies.

- **Program Acceptance Documents**  
  Official acceptance letter or confirmation from the host institution.

---

## 🗂 2. Submission Format  
- PDF format preferred  
- English or Japanese accepted  
- Ensure all documents are legible  
- Keep digital and printed copies for travel

---

## 📬 3. How to Submit  
- Upload documents through the designated portal  
- Confirm successful upload  
- Update documents if any changes occur  
- Contact your coordinator if issues arise
