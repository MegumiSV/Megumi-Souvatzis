# 🔧 Outbound Student Preparation Workflow

This workflow outlines the internal procedures for preparing outbound students for study abroad programs.  
It is designed for staff use and ensures consistent, accurate, and timely support.

---

## 🟦 1. Application & Eligibility Check

### Staff Tasks
- Review student application form  
- Confirm GPA and language requirements  
- Verify program eligibility  
- Notify student of acceptance  

### Output
- Student added to outbound tracking list  
- Program acceptance email sent  

---

## 🟦 2. Document Collection

### Required Documents
- Pledge form  
- Waiver & risk acknowledgment  
- Insurance certificate  
- Flight ticket information  
- Emergency contact sheet  

### Staff Tasks
- Check completeness and readability  
- Confirm insurance coverage meets requirements  
- Verify flight itinerary  
- Store documents in student folder  

### Output
- “Documents Complete” status updated  

---

## 🟦 3. Pre‑Departure Briefing

### Staff Tasks
- Schedule briefing session  
- Prepare slides and materials  
- Review safety guidelines  
- Explain local laws and cultural expectations  
- Provide emergency contact information  

### Output
- Student marked as “Briefing Completed”  

---

## 🟦 4. Insurance Verification

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm medical, evacuation, liability coverage  
- Check policy dates match travel period  
- Contact student if corrections are needed  

### Output
- Insurance marked as “Approved”  

---

## 🟦 5. Flight Ticket Confirmation

### Staff Tasks
- Verify departure/arrival dates  
- Check transit locations  
- Confirm itinerary matches program schedule  
- Update travel database  

### Output
- Travel details added to outbound master list  

---

## 🟦 6. Emergency Contact Registration

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm primary & secondary contacts  
- Verify phone numbers and email formats  
- Store contacts in emergency database  

### Output
- Emergency contacts marked as “Verified”  

---

## 🟦 7. Final Approval

### Staff Tasks
- Review all documents  
- Confirm briefing attendance  
- Ensure insurance and flight details are approved  
- Issue final clearance email  

### Output
- Student marked as “Ready for Departure”  

---

## 🛡 Notes

- Update workflow steps whenever procedures change  
- Keep all outbound workflow files within the `workflow/` directory  
- Maintain consistent communication with students throughout the process
