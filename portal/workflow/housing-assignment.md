# 🏠 Housing Assignment Workflow

This workflow outlines the internal procedures for assigning housing to inbound students.  
It ensures accurate placement, smooth move‑in coordination, and consistent communication with students and housing partners.

---

## 🟩 1. Collect Housing Preferences

### Staff Tasks
- Review student housing preference form  
- Confirm preferred housing type (dormitory, apartment, homestay)  
- Check special requests (dietary needs, accessibility, quiet environment)  
- Store form in student housing folder  

### Output
- Preferences marked as “Received”

---

## 🟩 2. Check Availability

### Staff Tasks
- Review dormitory room availability  
- Confirm private apartment options  
- Coordinate with homestay providers  
- Match availability with student preferences  

### Output
- Availability marked as “Checked”

---

## 🟩 3. Assign Housing Type

### Staff Tasks
- Select best match based on preferences and availability  
- Consider budget, commute distance, and program duration  
- Flag students requiring special accommodation  
- Record assigned housing type in database  

### Output
- Housing type marked as “Assigned”

---

## 🟩 4. Allocate Specific Room / Placement

### Dormitory Tasks
- Assign room number  
- Prepare key or access card  
- Confirm move‑in date  

### Apartment Tasks
- Confirm landlord contact  
- Prepare contract documents  
- Verify utilities setup  

### Homestay Tasks
- Match student with host family  
- Confirm host profile and expectations  
- Provide host contact information  

### Output
- Placement marked as “Allocated”

---

## 🟩 5. Send Housing Confirmation to Student

### Staff Tasks
- Prepare housing confirmation email  
- Include:
  - Housing type  
  - Address  
  - Move‑in date  
  - Contact information  
  - What to bring  
- Attach housing guidelines (if applicable)

### Output
- Student marked as “Housing Confirmed”

---

## 🟩 6. Prepare Move‑In Support

### Staff Tasks
- Prepare welcome materials  
- Coordinate airport pickup (if available)  
- Provide transportation instructions  
- Ensure room is ready (dormitory only)

### Output
- Move‑in support marked as “Prepared”

---

## 🟩 7. Move‑In Day Coordination

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm student arrival  
- Provide keys/access card  
- Assist with basic setup (Wi‑Fi, orientation materials)  
- Answer initial questions  

### Output
- Student marked as “Moved In”

---

## 🟩 8. Post Move‑In Follow‑Up

### Staff Tasks
- Check if student has settled in  
- Address any housing issues  
- Coordinate repairs or adjustments if needed  
- Update housing status to “Completed”

### Output
- Housing assignment marked as “Finalized”

---

## 🛡 Notes

- Update housing availability regularly  
- Maintain communication with dormitory staff and homestay partners  
- Store all housing documents securely in the student’s folder
