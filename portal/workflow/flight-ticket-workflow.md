# 🛫 Flight Ticket Confirmation Workflow

This workflow outlines the internal procedure for verifying outbound student flight itineraries.  
It ensures accurate travel information, emergency coordination, and alignment with partner university schedules.

---

## 🟦 1. Receive Flight Ticket Submission

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm file format (PDF, JPEG, PNG)
- Check readability of itinerary details
- Store the file in the student’s travel folder

### Output
- Flight ticket marked as “Received”

---

## 🟦 2. Verify Departure Information

### Staff Tasks
- Check departure airport  
- Confirm date and time  
- Verify airline and flight number  
- Ensure departure date aligns with program start

### Output
- Departure details marked as “Verified”

---

## 🟦 3. Verify Arrival Information

### Staff Tasks
- Check arrival airport  
- Confirm arrival date and time  
- Verify airline and flight number  
- Ensure arrival timing matches orientation schedule

### Output
- Arrival details marked as “Verified”

---

## 🟦 4. Check Transit / Layover Details

### Staff Tasks
- Review transit airports  
- Confirm layover duration is reasonable  
- Verify connecting flight numbers  
- Ensure no risky or extremely short connections

### Output
- Transit details marked as “Checked”

---

## 🟦 5. Confirm Consistency with Insurance Dates

### Staff Tasks
- Compare travel dates with insurance coverage period  
- Ensure coverage includes transit days  
- Contact student if insurance dates need adjustment

### Output
- Travel dates marked as “Aligned with Insurance”

---

## 🟦 6. Update Travel Database

### Staff Tasks
- Enter departure/arrival details  
- Add transit information  
- Link itinerary to student profile  
- Flag any unusual travel patterns (e.g., long layovers)

### Output
- Travel database updated

---

## 🟦 7. Request Corrections (If Needed)

### Staff Tasks
- Notify student of missing or incorrect details  
- Provide specific correction points  
- Set deadline for resubmission  
- Re-check updated itinerary

### Output
- Student marked as “Pending Correction”

---

## 🟦 8. Final Approval

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm all itinerary details  
- Ensure alignment with program schedule  
- Mark student as “Flight Approved”  
- Send confirmation email

### Output
- Student marked as “Ready for Departure”

---

## 🛡 Notes

- Always store flight documents securely  
- Update workflow if partner universities change arrival requirements  
- Maintain consistent communication with students
