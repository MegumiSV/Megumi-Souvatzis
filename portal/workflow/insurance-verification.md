# 🔧 Insurance Verification Workflow

This workflow outlines the internal procedure for verifying student insurance coverage for both outbound and inbound programs.  
It ensures that all students meet university and partner institution requirements for medical and emergency protection.

---

## 🟦 1. Receive Insurance Certificate

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm the file format (PDF, JPEG, PNG)
- Check readability of all text
- Store the file in the student’s document folder

### Output
- Insurance document marked as “Received”

---

## 🟦 2. Verify Mandatory Coverage

### Required Coverage
- Medical treatment  
- Hospitalization  
- Emergency evacuation  
- Repatriation  
- Personal liability  

### Staff Tasks
- Check policy details against required coverage list
- Confirm coverage amounts meet minimum standards
- Verify coverage dates match the student’s travel/study period

### Output
- Coverage marked as “Meets Requirements” or “Needs Correction”

---

## 🟦 3. Confirm Policy Validity

### Staff Tasks
- Check policy start and end dates
- Ensure coverage includes transit days
- Verify international coverage (not domestic-only)
- Confirm insurer contact information is provided

### Output
- Policy marked as “Valid” or “Invalid”

---

## 🟦 4. Emergency Hotline Verification

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm 24/7 emergency hotline availability
- Check language support (English required)
- Verify hotline number is clearly listed

### Output
- Hotline marked as “Verified”

---

## 🟦 5. Request Corrections (If Needed)

### Staff Tasks
- Contact student with specific correction points
- Provide examples of acceptable insurance plans
- Set a deadline for resubmission

### Output
- Student marked as “Pending Correction”

---

## 🟦 6. Final Approval

### Staff Tasks
- Re-check corrected documents (if applicable)
- Confirm all required coverage is included
- Update student status to “Insurance Approved”
- Notify student of approval

### Output
- Student marked as “Cleared for Travel/Arrival”

---

## 🛡 Notes

- Always store insurance documents securely  
- Update workflow steps if partner institutions change requirements  
- Maintain consistency across outbound and inbound verification processes
