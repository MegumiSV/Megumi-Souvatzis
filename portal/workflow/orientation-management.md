# 🎓 Orientation Management Workflow

This workflow outlines the internal procedures for planning, preparing, and delivering orientation for inbound students.  
It ensures consistent communication, accurate information, and a smooth introduction to campus life.

---

## 🟩 1. Prepare Orientation Schedule

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm orientation dates based on academic calendar  
- Coordinate with departments (academic affairs, health center, library, student support)  
- Create a draft schedule including:
  - Welcome session  
  - Administrative procedures  
  - Academic guidance  
  - Safety training  
  - Campus tour  
- Finalize schedule and upload to portal  

### Output
- Orientation schedule marked as “Prepared”

---

## 🟩 2. Prepare Orientation Materials

### Staff Tasks
- Update slides and handouts  
- Prepare campus maps  
- Print welcome packets  
- Prepare emergency contact cards  
- Ensure materials are available in English (and Japanese if needed)

### Output
- Materials marked as “Ready”

---

## 🟩 3. Register Students for Sessions

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm inbound student list  
- Assign students to orientation groups (if large cohort)  
- Register students for mandatory sessions  
- Send confirmation email with:
  - Orientation schedule  
  - Meeting locations  
  - Required documents to bring  

### Output
- Students marked as “Registered”

---

## 🟩 4. Coordinate Administrative Procedures

### Required Tasks
- City hall registration  
- National Health Insurance enrollment  
- Bank account setup  
- Student ID card issuance  

### Staff Tasks
- Prepare instructions for each procedure  
- Coordinate with city hall or partner offices  
- Schedule group visits (if applicable)  
- Ensure students know what documents to bring  

### Output
- Administrative tasks marked as “Coordinated”

---

## 🟩 5. Conduct Orientation Sessions

### Staff Tasks
- Welcome students and introduce staff  
- Present academic and campus information  
- Explain classroom etiquette and grading system  
- Provide safety and emergency training  
- Lead campus tour  

### Output
- Orientation marked as “Completed”

---

## 🟩 6. Post‑Orientation Follow‑Up

### Staff Tasks
- Send follow‑up email with key reminders  
- Provide links to campus resources  
- Confirm completion of administrative procedures  
- Address student questions or concerns  
- Update student status to “Orientation Completed”

### Output
- Follow‑up marked as “Sent”

---

## 🟩 7. Record Keeping

### Staff Tasks
- Store attendance records  
- Save orientation materials for next semester  
- Update workflow documents if procedures change  

### Output
- Orientation records marked as “Archived”

---

## 🛡 Notes

- Orientation is mandatory for all inbound students  
- Update materials every semester  
- Maintain clear communication before, during, and after orientation
