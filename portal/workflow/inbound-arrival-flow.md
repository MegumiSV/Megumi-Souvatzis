# 🔧 Inbound Arrival Coordination Workflow

This workflow outlines the internal procedures for supporting inbound students from the moment they arrive in Japan until they complete initial campus setup.  
It ensures smooth arrival, proper registration, and timely access to essential services.

---

## 🟩 1. Receive Arrival Information

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm flight itinerary submission  
- Check arrival date/time  
- Verify airport (Narita, Haneda, Kansai, etc.)  
- Store itinerary in student arrival folder  

### Output
- Arrival information marked as “Received”

---

## 🟩 2. Prepare Arrival Support

### Staff Tasks
- Check if airport pickup is available  
- Assign support staff (if applicable)  
- Prepare arrival instructions (transportation, meeting points)  
- Send arrival guidance email to student  

### Output
- Arrival support marked as “Prepared”

---

## 🟩 3. Arrival Day Coordination

### Staff Tasks
- Monitor student arrival time  
- Provide emergency contact number  
- Confirm student arrival at campus or housing  
- Assist with transportation questions if needed  

### Output
- Student marked as “Arrived in Japan”

---

## 🟩 4. Housing Move‑In Support

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm dormitory assignment  
- Prepare move‑in instructions  
- Provide room key or access card  
- Assist with basic setup (Wi‑Fi, orientation materials)  

### Output
- Housing status marked as “Moved In”

---

## 🟩 5. City Hall Registration

### Required Tasks (within 14 days)
- Address registration  
- My Number notification issuance  
- National Health Insurance enrollment (if required)

### Staff Tasks
- Provide registration instructions  
- Accompany students (if support is available)  
- Check completion and store documents  

### Output
- City hall registration marked as “Completed”

---

## 🟩 6. Residence Card Verification

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm student received residence card at airport  
- Check card details (name, status, validity)  
- Store copy in student file  

### Output
- Residence card marked as “Verified”

---

## 🟩 7. Mobile Phone & Banking Support

### Staff Tasks
- Provide SIM card setup guidance  
- Assist with mobile plan selection  
- Support bank account opening  
- Ensure student can access online banking  

### Output
- Mobile & banking setup marked as “Completed”

---

## 🟩 8. Orientation Preparation

### Staff Tasks
- Confirm orientation schedule  
- Prepare welcome materials  
- Register student for sessions  
- Provide campus map and safety information  

### Output
- Orientation status marked as “Ready”

---

## 🟩 9. Final Arrival Confirmation

### Staff Tasks
- Verify completion of all arrival steps  
- Update student status to “Arrival Completed”  
- Notify academic advisors and relevant departments  

### Output
- Student marked as “Fully Registered”

---

## 🛡 Notes

- Update workflow steps if immigration or university policies change  
- Maintain consistent communication with students during arrival week  
- Store all arrival documents securely in the student’s folder
